1) ROI / Payback Calculator (Free with Pro outputs)

Purpose: Quick investment viability check + cashflow projection.

Inputs (form fields):
	•	Purchase price (currency)
	•	Down payment (currency or %)
	•	Loan amount (currency) — auto-calc if down payment entered
	•	Interest rate (annual %)
	•	Loan term (years)
	•	Gross monthly revenue (currency)
	•	Other monthly income (currency)
	•	Monthly operating expenses (currency) — allow line-item expand (utilities, payroll, supplies, maintenance)
	•	Vacancy factor / shrinkage (%) — optional
	•	Capital expenditure per year (currency) — optional

Formulas / Logic:
	•	monthlyDebt = PMT(annualRate/12, loanTerm*12, loanAmount)
	•	netMonthlyIncome = grossMonthlyRevenue + otherIncome - monthlyExpenses - monthlyDebt
	•	annualNetIncome = netMonthlyIncome * 12
	•	ROI% = (annualNetIncome / downPayment) * 100 (if downPayment = 0, show ROI on cashflow basis or use cash-on-cash handling)
	•	paybackYears = purchasePrice / annualNetIncome (if annualNetIncome <= 0 show “no payback”)
	•	IRR (optional advanced): estimate using multi-year cashflow table (yearly net income, capex) — Pro-only output

Outputs:
	•	Monthly net income (currency)
	•	Annual net income
	•	ROI %
	•	Payback period (years)
	•	5–10 year cashflow projection chart
	•	Alert flags: negative net income, debt coverage ratio < 1.2

Freemium gating:
	•	Free: Shows Monthly Net, Annual Net, basic ROI %, payback estimate & simple chart (first 3 years).
	•	Pro / One-time report: Full 10-year cashflow, IRR, sensitivity analysis (interest rate / vend price / turns), downloadable PDF.

UI copy (CTA):
	•	Free: “Run a quick ROI check — see month-to-month profit now”
	•	Upsell: “Want the full 10-year forecast and IRR? Generate a CLEANBI Report for $59 or get unlimited reports in Pro.”

Implementation notes:
	•	Use JS PMT formula for monthlyDebt.
	•	Cache calculation outputs to user’s Firestore doc if logged in.
	•	Allow upload of historical monthly P&L to seed real revenue inputs (Pro).

2) Turn-Per-Day Benchmark Tool (Free)

Purpose: Benchmarks washers per-turn performance and revenue per machine.

Inputs:
	•	Machine type / washer size (dropdown: 20lb, 30lb, 40lb, 60lb, 80lb)
	•	Count of washers by size (allow multiple)
	•	Average turns per pair/per machine per day (numeric)
	•	Vend price (per turn currency)
	•	Operating days per year (default 312 / allow edit)
	•	Add-on revenue (drop-off, soap vend, change machine income)

Formulas:
	•	annualRevenuePerMachine = avgTurnsPerDay * vendPrice * operatingDays
	•	storeAnnualRevenue = Σ (annualRevenuePerMachine * machineCount) + addOnRevenue
	•	performanceIndex = avgTurnsPerDay / benchmarkAvg (benchmarks below)
	•	recommendedVendChange = function of performanceIndex + local avg price (Pro advanced)

Benchmarks (default, editable):
	•	20lb: 3.5 turns/day average
	•	30lb: 4 turns/day
	•	40lb: 4.5
	•	60lb: 5
	•	80lb: 5.5

Outputs:
	•	Annual revenue per machine, store revenue estimate
	•	Machine performance grade (Under / Average / Above)
	•	Suggest vend price tests and expected revenue delta
	•	Chart: machine mix vs revenue contribution

Freemium gating:
	•	Free: Full revenue per machine and grade.
	•	Pro: Multi-scenario optimizer (A/B vend pricing tests), competitive benchmarking from area (requires Demographic/Mapbox overlay).

UI copy (CTA):
	•	“Check how your machines perform against industry benchmarks — free.”
	•	Upsell: “Run pricing experiments and see revenue uplift (Pro)”

Implementation notes:
	•	Allow templates for common store machines to speed entry.
	•	Offer CSV import for machine inventory.

3) Lease Evaluation Tool (Free quick score; Pro deep-report)

Purpose: Score lease risk and surface red flags before committing.

Inputs (quick form):
	•	Lease term (years)
	•	Renewal options (# and terms)
	•	Base rent ($/month or $/sqft)
	•	NNN (costs per month or $/sqft)
	•	Rent escalation (annual %)
	•	CAM fees ($/month or $/sqft)
	•	Utilities responsibility (Landlord / Tenant / Shared)
	•	Termination clause (Tenant-friendly / Mutual / Landlord-friendly)
	•	Exclusive usage clause (Yes/No)
	•	Parking availability (Good/Adequate/Poor)

Scoring algorithm (normalized to 0–100):
	•	termScore (0–20): >=10 years → higher score
	•	renewalScore (0–15): number and favorability weight
	•	NNNScore (0–15): lower NNN => higher score
	•	escalationScore (0–10): lower escalation => higher score
	•	CAMScore (0–10)
	•	utilitiesScore (0–10): landlord pays => max
	•	terminationScore (0–10)
	•	parkingScore (0–10)

Compute weighted sum → totalLeaseScore

Outputs:
	•	Numeric score 0–100, Grade A–F
	•	Red flags list (if CAM > threshold, if no exclusivity, short term, high NNN)
	•	Suggested negotiation checklist (auto-populated)
	•	PDF lease summary (Pro)

Freemium gating:
	•	Free: Quick numeric score and top 3 red flags.
	•	Email-gated: full lease checklist downloadable (requires email).
	•	Pro: PDF + recommended clause language + example negotiation script + expert review booking.

UI copy (CTA):
	•	“Score your lease in 2 minutes — avoid the worst mistakes.”
	•	Upsell: “Need help negotiating? Get an expert review included with Pro.”

Implementation notes:

4) Demographic Suitability Analyzer (Email-gated, Pro overlays)

Purpose: Assess local demand fit using renter rates, income, density.

Inputs:
	•	Address or ZIP code
	•	Radius (default 1 mile)
	•	Target customer profile (renters / families / students)

Data sources:
	•	U.S. Census API (American Community Survey)
	•	ATTOM or other paid datasets for richer overlays (optional for Pro)
	•	Mapbox for spatial mapping and competitor density overlay

Computed metrics & formulas:
	•	renterPct = census.renter_percentage
	•	medianIncomeArea = census.median_household_income
	•	populationDensity = persons_per_sq_mile
	•	incomeFit = clamp( (medianIncomeArea / targetMedianIncome) * 100, 0–100)
	•	densityScore = normalize(populationDensity, market-specific scale)
	•	suitabilityScore = 0.4 * renterPct + 0.3 * incomeFit + 0.3 * densityScore → normalized 0–100

Outputs:
	•	Suitability Score (0–100), Grade A–F
	•	Renter % and median income callouts
	•	Suggested target mix and likely average vend price band
	•	Map: competitors + renter density heatmap (Pro)
	•	Competitor count within radius

Freemium gating:
	•	Basic: require email to view Suitability Score & basic stats.
	•	Pro: full map overlay, competitor rent rates, trend graphs, 5-year population growth projection.

UI copy (CTA):
	•	“Type the address — we’ll tell you if the neighborhood supports a laundromat.”
	•	Upsell: “Unlock competitor maps and rent rates in Pro.”

Implementation notes:
	•	Cache census lookups by ZIP/radius to avoid hitting rate limits.
	•	Offer credit-based paid API calls for ATTOM/Mapbox for Pro heavy users.

5) Equipment Cost & Replacement Planner (Free basic; Pro advanced)

Purpose: Help operators budget capex and plan equipment lifecycle.

Inputs:
	•	Machine brand & model (dropdown / searchable)
	•	Year installed or age
	•	Expected useful life (years)
	•	Current maintenance cost per year
	•	Estimated replacement cost (use database of manufacturer suggested pricing)
	•	Downtime cost per hour (entered by operator)

Formulas:
	•	annualDepreciation = replacementCost / usefulLife
	•	totalAnnualOwnershipCost = maintenanceCost + annualDepreciation
	•	expectedReplacementYear = currentYear + (usefulLife - age)
	•	ROI impact if replaced early = project uplift in turns * vendPrice - replacementCost amortized

Outputs:
	•	Annual ownership cost
	•	Suggested replacement year
	•	Replacement urgency flag (High/Medium/Low)
	•	Parts list with typical costs (Pro enriched with parts supplier integration)

Freemium gating:
	•	Free: simple replacement schedule and ownership cost.
	•	Pro: integrated parts cost lookup, supplier links, one-click order list, predicted revenue uplift if replaced.

UI copy (CTA):
	•	“See when your machines will bite you — and what replacing them will cost vs return.”
	•	Upsell: “Want a parts-ready kit + supplier pricing? That’s Pro.”

Implementation notes:
	•	Maintain equipment price database; seed with common commercial brands (Speed Queen, Dexter, Huebsch, Electrolux).
	•	Allow manual override fields.

6) Utility Cost Optimizer (Free + Pro energy plan)

Purpose: Show utilities savings by switching cycles, load balancing, or efficient machines.

Inputs:
	•	Average cycles per day / month (washers, dryers)
	•	Utility rates ($/kWh, $/therm, water $/gallon)
	•	Machine efficiency (gallons per cycle, kWh per cycle) — default per model or manual entry
	•	Peak demand charges (if any)

Formulas:
	•	monthlyConsumption = Σ(machineCount * cyclesPerMonth * consumptionPerCycle)
	•	monthlyUtilityCost = monthlyConsumption * unitRates
	•	projectedSavings = compare current machines to energy-efficient alternatives (use manufacturer efficiency deltas)
	•	ROI on upgrade = replacementCost / annualUtilitySavings

Outputs:
	•	Monthly utility cost breakdown (water, electric, gas)
	•	Suggested efficiency moves (LED, efficient washers, dryer sensors)
	•	Payback on efficiency upgrade

Freemium gating:
	•	Free: basic monthly utility cost
	•	Pro: suggested efficiency kit + modeled ROI + vendor financing partners

UI copy (CTA):
	•	“Cut utility bills — run your store through our optimizer.”
	•	Upsell: “Pro gives you manufacturer pricing + financing options.”

Implementation notes:
	•	For Energy-efficient alternatives, keep a small seeded DB per model.
	•	Integrate with utility data where available.

7) CLEANBI Composite Report (Paid / Pro)

Purpose: Single authoritative paid report that bundles ROI, Lease, Demographics, Turns, Equipment, and Utility analysis into a polished PDF with CLEANBI score and recommendations.

Inputs:
	•	Pulls all calculators’ inputs/outputs + uploaded P&Ls, photos, lease doc.
	•	Optional: owner-provided P&L CSV for higher fidelity.

CLEANBI Score algorithm (example weighting):
	•	Market Suitability (Demographics) — 25%
	•	Financial Health (ROI & EBITDA proxy) — 25%
	•	Lease Strength — 15%
	•	Equipment Condition & Age — 10%
	•	Competition Density & Turns — 15%
	•	Utility & Operating Risk — 10%
Normalize to 0–100. Map to grade (A: 85–100, B: 70–84, C: 55–69, D: 40–54, F: <40).

Outputs / PDF contents:
	•	Executive summary (one page)
	•	CLEANBI score and grade
	•	Key KPIs: Net income, EBITDA estimate, DSCR estimate, Payback Period
	•	Lease score and negotiation notes
	•	Market map (Mapbox) with competitors
	•	5-year cashflow projection & sensitivity table (vend price, turns, interest rate)
	•	Equipment summary & recommended parts/replacement
	•	Action plan: short term (90 days), medium (1 year), long (3 years)
	•	Suggested lenders & financing options linked (Eastern Funding, Dexter Financial, SBA resources)
	•	Optional: “Nick’s Notes” personalized recommendation (Pro auto-generated)

Pricing/Access:

	•	Pro subscribers: unlimited reports included

Delivery & gating:
