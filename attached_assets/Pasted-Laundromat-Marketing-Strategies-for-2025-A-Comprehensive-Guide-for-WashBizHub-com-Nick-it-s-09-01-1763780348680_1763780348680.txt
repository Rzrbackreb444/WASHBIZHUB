Based on industry standards (Cents, Turns, CleanCloud), your book (e.g., Chapter 8: Operations Mastery), and group polls (e.g., 65% prioritize TPD), here’s what’s missing:
1.  Turns Per Day (TPD) Calculator
	•  Purpose: Calculates cycles per washer/day to optimize revenue (key KPI in laundromats).
	•  Formula: TPD = Total Cycles / Number of Washers / Operating Days
		•  Revenue Impact: Revenue = TPD × Vend Price × Days (350-365)
		•  Breakeven TPD: Breakeven TPD = Fixed Costs / (Vend Price - $0.50-$1.00/turn)
		•  Score: TPD Score = clamp((TPD - 2) / (8 - 2) × 100, 0, 100) [Target: 4.5 urban, 4 rural]
	•  Depth: Include sensitivity (TPD ±10%), Monte Carlo (10,000 sims, mean=4.5, sd=0.5), and group benchmark (70k average 4.5).
	•  Tier: Free (basic), $19.99/mo (sensitivity), $49.99/mo (Monte Carlo).
2.  ROI Calculator
	•  Purpose: Estimates return on investment for equipment, stores.
	•  Formula: ROI = (NOI - Debt Service) / Equity
		•  NOI: Revenue - (Utilities + Labor + Rent + Maintenance)
		•  EBITDA: NOI - Owner Salary
		•  DSCR: NOI / (PMT × 12), PMT = (r/12 × Loan) / (1 - (1 + r/12)^(-years×12))
	•  Depth: Multi-year projections (3-5 years), equipment-specific (e.g., Dexter vs. Girbau), group case studies.
	•  Tier: Free (basic), $19.99/mo (projections), $49.99/mo (cases).
3.  Cash Flow Calculator
	•  Purpose: Tracks cash inflows/outflows for operational stability.
	•  Formula: Cash Flow = Cash Inflows - Cash Outflows
		•  Inflows: Revenue, loans
		•  Outflows: Expenses, repayments
	•  Depth: Monthly/annual views, sensitivity (revenue ±10%), group benchmarks.
	•  Tier: Free (monthly), $19.99/mo (annual), $49.99/mo (sensitivity).
4.  Equipment Cost Estimator
