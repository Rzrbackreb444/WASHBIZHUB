                              STANDALONE CALCULATORS (9)

1. TPD CALCULATOR (Turns Per Day)
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/TPDCalculator.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Max Turns/Day = (Operating Hours × 60) / Avg Cycle Time
   • Actual TPD = Max TPD × (Occupancy Rate / 100)
   • Daily Revenue = (Washers × TPD × Wash Price) + (Dryers × TPD × Dry Price)
   • Monthly Revenue = Daily Revenue × 30
   • Yearly Revenue = Daily Revenue × 365
   • Monthly Expenses = (Daily Expenses × 30) + Rent + Utilities
   • Break-Even TPD = Monthly Expenses / (Avg Vend Price × Machine Count × 30)

2. ROI ANALYZER
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/ROIAnalyzer.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Monthly Payment = PMT(rate, term, loan_amount)
   • Year Revenue = Monthly Revenue × 12 × (1 + Growth Rate)^(year-1)
   • Year Expenses = Monthly Expenses × 12 × (1.02)^(year-1)
   • Year Cash Flow = Revenue - Expenses - Debt Service
   • NPV = Σ(Cash Flow / (1 + Discount Rate)^year)
   • IRR = Newton-Raphson iteration for NPV = 0
   • Cash-on-Cash Return = (Annual Cash Flow / Down Payment) × 100
   • Payback Period = First year where cumulative > 0

3. EQUIPMENT PAYBACK CALCULATOR
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/EquipmentPayback.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Total Cost = Equipment Cost + Installation + Shipping
   • Monthly Payment = PMT(rate/12, months, loan_amount)
   • Monthly Cash Flow = Revenue - Payment - (Annual Maintenance / 12)
   • Payback Months = First month where cumulative ≥ 0
   • Total Interest = (Monthly Payment × Payments) - Loan Amount
   • Equipment ROI = ((Annual Revenue - Maintenance - Payments) / Total Cost) × 100

4. FINANCING CALCULATOR PRO
   File: client/src/pages/FinancingCalculator.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Monthly Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
   • Total Interest = (Payment × Months) - Principal
   • Total Cost of Ownership = Principal + Interest + Fees
   • Effective APR = (Total Interest / Principal) / Years × 100
   • DSCR Required = NOI / Annual Debt Service ≥ 1.25

5. WATER USAGE INCOME VERIFIER
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/WaterUsageVerifier.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Estimated Gallons = Water Bill Amount / Cost Per Gallon
   • Loads Per Month = Gallons / Gallons Per Load
   • Calculated Revenue = Loads × Average Vend Price
   • Variance = ((Claimed - Calculated) / Claimed) × 100
   • Turns Per Day = Loads / (Machine Count × 30)
   • Utilization Rate = (Actual TPD / Max TPD) × 100
   • Fraud Risk = HIGH if variance > 30%, LOW if < 15%

6. REVENUE FORECASTER PRO
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/RevenueForecaster.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Base Monthly = Machine Count × TPD × Avg Vend × 30
   • Seasonal Adjustment = Base × Seasonal Factor (0.85-1.15)
   • Growth Projection = Current × (1 + Growth Rate)^months
   • Best Case = Projection × 1.15
   • Worst Case = Projection × 0.85

7. PRICING OPTIMIZER
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/PricingOptimizer.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Optimal Price = Cost + (Market Rate × Elasticity Factor)
   • Revenue Impact = (New Price - Current) × Volume × (1 - Elasticity)
   • Competitive Position = Your Price / Market Average
   • Margin = (Price - Variable Cost) / Price × 100

8. ROI CALCULATOR PRO
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/ROICalculatorPro.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • 5-Year ROI = ((Total Returns - Investment) / Investment) × 100
   • Annualized Return = ((1 + Total ROI)^(1/years) - 1) × 100
   • Break-Even Point = Investment / Annual Net Income

9. TURNS PER DAY CALCULATOR
   File: client/src/pages/calculators/TurnsPerDay.tsx
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formulas:
   • Theoretical Max TPD = Operating Hours / Cycle Time
   • Realistic TPD = Max × Utilization Factor
   • Revenue Per Turn = Avg Vend Price
   • Daily Capacity Revenue = Machines × TPD × Vend Price

CLEANBI™ SCORE CALCULATION (17 weighted factors)
   File: client/src/lib/algorithms.ts
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Weights:
   • Rent (10%) = 100 - normalize(rent%, 10-40%)
   • EBITDA (10%) = normalize(margin, 10-35%)
   • Turns/Day (10%) = normalize(TPD, 2-6)
   • Saturation (8%) = 100 - normalize(competitors/density)
   • DSCR (8%) = normalize(ratio, 1.1-2.0)
   • Renters (6%) = normalize(%, 20-70%)
   • Density (6%) = normalize(HH/sqmi, 500-5000)
   • Traffic (6%) = direct score 0-100
   • Equipment Mix (6%) = direct score 0-100
   • Income (5%) = 100 - normalize(|income - $55K|)
   • Utilities (5%) = 100 - normalize(%, 8-20%)
   • Parking (4%) = direct score 0-100
   • Cashless (4%) = 100 if yes, 40 if no
   • WDF Space (4%) = normalize(sqft, 0-500)
   • Household Size (3%) = normalize(size, 2.2-3.6)
   • Delivery Ready (3%) = 100 if yes, 50 if no
   • Curbside Ready (2%) = 100 if yes, 50 if no

   Letter Grade:
   • A = 90+, B = 80-89, C = 70-79, D = 60-69, F = <60

FINANCE FORMULAS
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • ROI % = (Net Profit / Invested) × 100
   • Payback Years = Invested / Annual Net (or null if <0)
   • DSCR = EBITDA / Debt Service
   • EBITDA Margin = (EBITDA / Revenue) × 100

SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS (1D Sweeps)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Tests ±10% changes on any single input
   • Returns CLEANBI score impact for each scenario

WHAT-IF SIMULATOR (Multi-Variable)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Applies multiple deltas simultaneously
   • Shock Presets:
     - Utility Spike: +20% utilities
     - Competitor Opens: +50% competition
     - Machine Downtime: -10% TPD
     - Rent Increase: +8% rent
     - Price Cut: -5% EBITDA
     - Vend Increase: +3% EBITDA

DELTA ATTRIBUTION (Shapley-Lite)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Identifies which factor moved score most
   • One-at-a-time contribution analysis
   • Normalized to total delta

MONTE CARLO SIMULATION
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • 500 random draws (configurable)
   • Uniform distribution within specified ranges
   • Returns: min, p10, p25, median, p75, p90, max, mean

REPAIR OR REPLACE DECISION
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Formula: If (Repair Cost + Lost Revenue) < (40% × New Machine Cost)
   • Then "Repair", else "Replace"
   • Lost Revenue = (Annual Profit / 365) × Downtime Days

SATURATION SCORE
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Saturation Raw = Competitors / (HH Density / 1000)
   • Score = 100 - normalize(raw, 0.1-2.0)
