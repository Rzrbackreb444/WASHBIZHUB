2. Valuation & Calculators → Lender-Ready
Current: 50+ calculators (ROI, TPD, Loan, etc.), but informal outputs.
Key Upgrades Needed & Recommendations:
•  Scenario Saving & Sensitivity: Add side-by-side views (Conservative: -10% revenue; Aggressive: +15% turns) with matrices showing impacts on ROI/valuation.
•  Lender-Ready Exports: Professional PDFs with branded formatting, assumption logs (e.g., “SDE multiple: 4x based on industry avg”), and charts.
•  Persona Presets: Defaults like “First-Time Buyer” (higher equity req.) vs. “Investor” (aggressive growth).
•  Industry Standards: Common methods include Revenue Multiple (0.5-1x gross), SDE Multiple (3-5x), Asset-Based (equipment value), and Market Comps. Lenders (esp. SBA) require verified financials and future cash flow projections. Sources emphasize professional appraisals for SBA loans to avoid disputes.
Banks accept outputs that mirror certified valuations — this bridges to funding approvals.
