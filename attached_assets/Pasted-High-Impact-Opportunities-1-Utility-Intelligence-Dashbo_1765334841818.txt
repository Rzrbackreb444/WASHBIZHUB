High-Impact Opportunities
1. Utility Intelligence Dashboard
Real-time tracking of water, gas, electricity costs per machine/wash cycle. The industry's biggest variable cost with no good tools to optimize it.

Cost-per-pound calculator
Utility rate comparison by region
ROI calculator for high-efficiency upgrades
Anomaly detection (leak alerts, equipment malfunction)
2. Route Optimization for Pickup & Delivery (PUD)
The wash-dry-fold pickup/delivery segment is exploding. No good route planning tools exist for laundromat operators.

Google Maps integration for optimized routes
Customer scheduling portal
Driver app with turn-by-turn
Delivery zone profitability analysis
3. Staffing & Payroll Calculator
Labor is the #2 expense. Operators guess at scheduling.

Revenue-per-labor-hour benchmarks
Optimal staffing by time-of-day/day-of-week
Break-even calculator for attended vs. unattended
Payroll integration
4. Insurance & Compliance Hub
Every operator needs it, nobody understands it.

Coverage comparison tool
Premium calculator by location/equipment value
ADA compliance checklist
Fire code requirements by state
Document vault for certificates
5. Customer Loyalty & Retention System
Missing from most laundromat tech stacks.

Digital punch cards
SMS marketing campaigns
Review solicitation automation
Customer lifetime value calculator
Churn prediction (for PUD customers)
6. Equipment Lifecycle Manager
Track every machine from purchase to retirement.

Depreciation schedules
Maintenance history
Repair vs. replace calculator
Resale value estimator (feeds into marketplace)
Warranty tracking
7. Water Reclamation ROI Calculator
Environmental regulations are tightening. Green laundromats get incentives.

Payback period for reclaim systems
Local rebate finder
Before/after utility comparison
8. Competitor Intelligence Tool
Operators want to know what's around them.

Map of nearby laundromats with machine counts
Google review sentiment analysis
Price comparison by neighborhood
Market saturation scoring
