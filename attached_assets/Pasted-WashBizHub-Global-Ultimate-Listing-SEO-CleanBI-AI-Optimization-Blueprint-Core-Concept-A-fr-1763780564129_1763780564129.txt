2. AI Agents (All-in-One Multi-Agent System)

Agent Types & Roles:
	•	SEO Agent:
	•	Keyword research & trending search optimization
	•	Monitors SERP position, backlinks, indexing status
	•	Auto-generates meta titles, descriptions, schema, alt text
	•	Content Agent:
	•	Auto-generates blogs & articles for each listing
	•	Handles internal linking between listings and blog posts
	•	Produces social posts, newsletters, and linkable content
	•	Marketing & Conversion Agent:
	•	Optimizes price and sale timing using CleanBI + Monte Carlo simulations
	•	Suggests cross-promotions, campaigns, and buyer targeting
	•	CleanBI Agent:
	•	Computes location, foot traffic, competition, demographics, lease quality, revenue potential
	•	Produces automated scoring (A-F) for buyers and sellers
	•	Generates valuation recommendations
	•	Heatmap & Competitive Intelligence Agent:
	•	Visualizes competition, saturation, and underserved markets
	•	Tracks new competitors in real-time
	•	Predictive analytics for market entry
	•	Finance & Valuation Agent:
	•	ROI, DSCR, EBITDA, equity returns
	•	Monte Carlo risk simulations for cashflow & price projections

Multi-Agent Collaboration:
Agents talk to each other via an internal reasoning engine, prioritizing highest-impact actions for listings: e.g., if CleanBI suggests undervalued property, Marketing Agent boosts visibility, SEO Agent optimizes SERP, Content Agent generates blogs targeting top buyer search terms.

3. CleanBI Scoring & Valuation

Global Commercial Intelligence Score (CleanBI 2.0): CLEANBI = 0.22 × MarketScore + 0.22 × FinancialScore + 0.14 × LeaseScore + 0.14 × CompetitionScore + 0.12 × EquipmentScore + 0.08 × UtilitiesScore + 0.08 × ReadinessScore
Normalize 0-100:
A (85-100): Elite investable
B (70-84): Negotiate
C (55-69): Needs work
D/F (<55): High risk Monte Carlo Simulations:
	•	Revenue & TPD variability ±10%
	•	Pricing ±10%
	•	DSCR probability >1.25
	•	Cashflow risk assessment
	•	Equipment Valuation:
	•	Depreciation schedules per brand/type
	•	Replacement cost & ROI on upgrades
	•	Predictive maintenance forecasts
	•	Global Demographics & Competition:
	•	Google Maps + Popular Times for foot traffic
	•	US Census or local census equivalent
	•	Competitor density via Google Places
