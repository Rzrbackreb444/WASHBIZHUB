Chapter 3: Location Is Law – Demographics and CLEANBI Scoring
Wrong spot, you’re sunk—location’s 80%. Hunt: 1,500+ renters/mile, main road, 1.5 parking spaces/washer, $30K-65K income, <3 rivals. C.L.E.A.N.: Community (demo fit), Lease (terms), Equipment (mix), Accessibility (parking), Numbers (rent <25%, EBITDA). CLEANBI Score: Rent ratio (15%), turns (20%), parking (10%), income (15%), utilities (10%), competition (15%), demos (10%), EBITDA (15%). 90-100 = elite; <60 = walk. 2025: 60% renters in urban hubs. Red flags: High crime (police reports), declining areas (census trends). Consult WashBizHub.com for CLEANBI analysis.
CLEANBI Formula: CLEANBI = 0.22 × MarketScore + 0.22 × FinancialScore + 0.14 × LeaseScore + 0.14 × CompetitionScore + 0.12 × EquipmentScore + 0.08 × UtilitiesScore + 0.08 × ReadinessScore. Normalize 0-100: e.g., TPD = clamp((TPD - 2) / (8 - 2) × 100, 0, 100). Grades: A (85-100, invest), B (70-84, negotiate), C (55-69, fix), D/F (<55, high risk). Tune weights for urban/rural.
Chapter 4: Lease Logic – Negotiation and Trap Avoidance
Bad lease = slow bleed. Laundromat123.com: Leases kill more than machines. Must-haves: 10+ years, rent <25% revenue, triple net clarity, exclusivity clause, ADA splits. Negotiate: TI allowance ($10K-50K), 3-6 free months, abatement, right of first refusal. Traps: Short terms, landlord HVAC costs. Attorney up—$2K saves $200K. X tip: Cap rent hikes at 2-3%/yr. Consult WashBizHub.com for lease reviews.

Chapter 5: Equipment Intelligence – Brands, Specs, Diagnostics
No brand’s king—fit your market. Breakdown (laundromat123.com, Coin Laundry Association, 70k FB group, X):
•  Speed Queen: Pros: 20-25yr life, high resale; Cons: Pricier ($5,500-$8,000). High-volume.
•  Dexter: Pros: Durable, Dexter Live cloud; Cons: Repair delays ($4,500-$6,500). Reliable.
•  Huebsch: Pros: Value, Speed Queen parts; Cons: Less resale ($4,800-$7,200). Budget.
•  Continental Girbau: Pros: Eco, low water (18 gal); Cons: Costly ($12,000-$22,000). Green markets.
•  Electrolux/Wascomat: Pros: Upscale; Cons: Parts cost ($6,000-$9,000). High-end.
•  Maytag: Pros: Reliable; Cons: Shorter life ($2,800-$3,500). Starters.
•  B&C: Pros: Cheap ($3,000-$5,000); Cons: Less durable. Low-budget.
•  LG: Pros: Efficient; Cons: Not 24/7 ($4,000-$6,000). Light use, campus stores.
Specs: Speed Queen front load: 40-60 lb, 28-35 min, 12 gal, $5,500-$8,000. Dexter: 40-60 lb, 30-38 min, 14 gal, $4,500-$6,500. Dryers: 1.5x washer capacity. Speed Queen stacks: 30 lb, $3,200-$4,000. Dexter singles: 50 lb, $2,400-$3,200.
Diagnostics/Repairs: IoT (Dexter Live: E01 = drain; Speed Queen E:11 = fill error) flags faults. Log downtime, repairs, costs. Stock belts, seals ($200-500/yr). Urgency = (age/12yr × 50) + (maintenance cost increase % × 0.5) + (downtime hrs/50 × 25). Grades: >80 (replace), 50-80 (consider), <50 (maintain). Schedule: Monthly vents, quarterly belts/seals, annual water tests. X tip: Keep parts onsite—delays kill.
Distributors: Northeast: Laundrylux (NY, laundrylux.com), Automated Laundry (MA). Southeast: Southeastern Laundry (AL/GA), Pierce (FL). Midwest: Coin-O-Matic (IL), Ed Brown (TX/OK). Chapter 8: Operations Mastery – Efficiency, POS, Maintenance
Profits spin when machines do. Efficiency: 4-6 TPD, clean vents (30% gain), water softeners (40% life), reclamation systems (40% water cut). Costs: Labor 15%, utilities 20-25%, rent 20%, maintenance 10%.
POS: Cents (delivery, $500-1,500 setup, $100/mo), Curbside (CRM, $1,000, $75/mo), FasCard (loyalty, $800, $50/mo), LaundryLux Connect (remote, $1,200, $80/mo), Dexter Live (diagnostics, $600, $60/mo).
Metrics:
•  TPD: Cycles/washer/day. Revenue = TPD × vend price × days (350-365). Breakeven TPD = fixed costs / (vend price - $0.50-1.00/turn). Score = clamp((TPD - 2) / (8 - 2) × 100, 0, 100). Target 4.5 urban, 4 rural.
•  EBITDA: NOI - owner salary. NOI = revenue - (utilities + labor + rent + maintenance). Margin = EBITDA/revenue (>20%). Score = clamp(margin / 0.4 × 100, 0, 100).
•  DSCR: NOI / (PMT × 12). PMT = (r/12 × loan) / (1 - (1 + r/12)^(-years×12)). Score = clamp(DSCR × 50, 0, 100). >1.25 safe.
•  ROI: (NOI - debt service) / equity. Sensitivity: Vary TPD ±10%, price ±10%. Example: TPD 4.5 to 4.95, price $3 to $3.30 → revenue +10%. Monte Carlo: 10,000 sims (TPD mean=4.5, sd=0.5; price mean=$3, sd=0.3). Output: P(DSCR >1.25) >80%. X tip: Smart meters catch 10% leaks. Consult WashBizHub.com for ops audits.
