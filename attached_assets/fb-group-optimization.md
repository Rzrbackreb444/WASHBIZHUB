**How to Make the Most of This Group:**

**1. Be Helpful First**
This community runs on shared knowledge. Answer questions when you can, share your experience, and support fellow operators. What goes around comes around.

**2. Post with Purpose**
When listing a laundromat or equipment:
- Include location, asking price, and photos
- Add key details (revenue, lease terms, equipment age)
- Be transparent — it builds trust

**3. Use Clear Formatting**
For diagnostics and repair questions:
- Include machine brand + model
- Describe the symptom clearly
- Share what you've already tried

**4. Connect with Vetted Vendors**
We have verified equipment distributors, service professionals, lenders, and suppliers in this group. Look for the Vendor Partner badges.

**5. Respect the Space**
No spam, no self-promotion without value, no drama. We keep it professional and productive.
