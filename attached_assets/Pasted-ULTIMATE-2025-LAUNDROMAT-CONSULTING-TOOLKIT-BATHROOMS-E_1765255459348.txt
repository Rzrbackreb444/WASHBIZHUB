**BATHROOM SECTION: ADA-COMPLIANT COMMERCIAL RESTROOMS FOR LAUNDROMATS (2025 STANDARDS – 2010 ADA + LOCAL CODES LIKE CA TITLE 24)**
Laundromats must have at least one ADA-compliant restroom (single or multi-user) per 1,000-1,500 sq ft public space (IBC/UD). Fines $4k+/violation; lawsuits common (Unruh Act in CA). Trends 2025: Combo bath-laundry hybrids (saves 20-30% space/cost), curbless walk-in showers, bold lighting (LED pendants $100-500). Designs: Minimalist (white tile, shaker vanities), eco (low-flow fixtures save $200/yr water). Integrate with laundry via stacked machines in 45-60 sq ft zones – utility sink doubles as laundry prep.

**ADA REQUIREMENTS (MUST-HAVES FOR ALL COMMERCIAL BATHS)**
- Turning Space: 60" diameter circle or T-shaped (60x60" sq) for wheelchair 180° turn [[0]](grok://citation?card_id=d05cad&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=0) [[2]](grok://citation?card_id=ab525e&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=2) [[6]](grok://citation?card_id=416f7b&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=6).
- Door: 32" min clear width, lever handles (5 lb max force), no swing into turning space [[4]](grok://citation?card_id=b27bcb&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=4) [[7]](grok://citation?card_id=5ed595&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=7).
- Toilet Stall (Accessible): 60" wide x 56" deep (wall-mount) or 59" deep (floor-mount); centerline 16-18" from side wall; height 17-19" seat [[1]](grok://citation?card_id=37e749&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=1) [[3]](grok://citation?card_id=d88223&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=3) [[6]](grok://citation?card_id=e3b44f&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=6).
- Grab Bars: Rear 36" L x 1.25-1.5" dia (33-36" AFF), side 42" L (split if flush valve); 1.5" clearance from wall [[0]](grok://citation?card_id=105d78&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=0) [[7]](grok://citation?card_id=4a6189&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=7).
- Sink (Lavatory): Max 34" rim height; 30x48" knee/toe clearance (27" high, 17" deep toe); insulated pipes; lever faucets (5 lb force) [[0]](grok://citation?card_id=52b17b&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=0) [[4]](grok://citation?card_id=e6ae02&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=4) [[8]](grok://citation?card_id=d3f7f5&card_type=citation_card&type=render_inline_citation&citation_id=8).
- Mirror: Bottom 40" max AFF, full width of sink.
- Flush Valve: 44" max AFF (side reach).
- At least 1 accessible stall per multi-user bath; signage (braille, 60" AFF).

**INTEGRATION WITH LAUNDRY: SERVICE/MAINT ACCESS**
- Rear W/D Space: 6-12" venting clearance (NFPA 54); 24-36" rear access (OSHA 1910.264) for belts/pipes – use fold-down panels ($200-$400).
- Plumbing Tie-In: Shared lines save $1k-$2k; GFCI outlets every 6 ft (NEC 210.8).
- Ventilation: Exhaust 50 CFM min (IMC); lint traps in combo sinks ($100-$300).
- Codes: Doors hold-open during service; 18" side gaps for techs.

**PYTHON EXPORT SCRIPT (COLAB-READY FOR PDF/EXCEL WITH BATHROOM DATA)**:
import pandas as pd
from reportlab.lib.pagesizes import letter
from reportlab.pdfgen import canvas

data = {
    'Category': ['ADA Turning', 'Door Width', 'Toilet Stall', 'Sink Height', 'Grab Bars', 'Combo Cost', 'Venting Rear'],
    'Details': ['60 dia', '32 min', '60x56 in', '34 max', '33-36 AFF', '$10-20k', '6-12 in'],
    '2025 Cost': ['$0', '$200-500', '$1-2k stall', '$300-600', '$200-500', '$10-20k', '$500-1.5k']
}

df = pd.DataFrame(data)
df.to_excel('Bathroom_Plan.xlsx', index=False)
df.to_csv('Bathroom_Plan.csv', index=False)
c = canvas.Canvas('Bathroom_Plan.pdf', pagesize=letter)
y = 750
for i, row in df.iterrows():
    c.drawString(100, y, f"{row['Category']}: {row['Details']} - Cost: {row['2025 Cost']}")
    y -= 20
c.save()
print("Exported: Bathroom_Plan.xlsx, .csv, .pdf – ADA-Compliant Ready!")
