📊 HOW SCORING WORKS

Every location gets scored 0-100 and graded A, B, or C based on:

• Foot Traffic — How many people pass by daily
• Demographics — Income levels, population density, growth trends
• Competition — Number and quality of nearby competitors
• Reviews — Google ratings and review sentiment
• Visibility — Street presence, signage potential, accessibility
• Market Demand — Search trends and local demand signals

A = Excellent investment opportunity (80-100)
B = Good investment opportunity (60-79)
C = Solid investment opportunity (40-59)
Below C = Needs work — proceed with caution
